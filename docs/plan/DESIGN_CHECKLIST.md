# Design checklist (FE-15)

All 13 PNGs in `desin/` were opened with the image viewer and compared with 390x844 headless-Chromium screenshots of the app running on mocks (`VITE_USE_MOCKS=true`). Mock data values (names, totals, counts, order number) differ from the PNGs by design and are not counted as differences. Screenshots are not committed; QA-07 produces the official ones.
